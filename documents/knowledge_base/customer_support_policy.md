# Customer Support SLA & Escalation Guidelines

**Document ID:** CS-POL-2024-V1  
**Department:** Customer Support & Experience  
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  

---

## 1. Response & Resolution SLA Tiers
- **Tier 1 (VIP & High-Value Customers):** First response within 15 minutes. Maximum resolution window of 4 business hours.
- **Tier 2 (Standard Priority):** First response within 2 hours. Maximum resolution window of 24 business hours.
- **Tier 3 (General Inquiries):** Response within 6 business hours.

## 2. Customer Escalation & Retention Vouchers
When customer interactions flag severe negative sentiment or repeated transit delays:
- Support leads are authorized to issue immediate apology store credit vouchers of ₹500 to ₹2,000.
- For unresolved technical complaints beyond 48 hours, customers receive complimentary doorstep inspection and direct ticket escalation to senior tier managers.
