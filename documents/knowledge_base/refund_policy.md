# Enterprise Refund & Return Policy

**Document ID:** POL-REF-2024-V3  
**Department:** Operations & Customer Experience  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** August 15, 2024  

---

## 1. General Return Window
Customers may request returns within **14 calendar days** of order delivery for eligible items. To qualify for a full refund or replacement, products must be in their original packaging with all accompanying accessories, manuals, and warranty cards intact.

## 2. Category-Specific Return & Refund Rules

### 2.1 Consumer Electronics & Computing
- **Eligibility:** 10-day replacement or return window for manufacturing defects, hardware failures, or transit damage.
- **Verification:** Mandatory technical verification by an authorized technician prior to refund approval.
- **Restocking Fee:** A 5% restocking fee applies if an opened item is returned without defects (customer remorse).

### 2.2 Home Appliances
- Large appliances (Air Conditioners, Washing Machines, Refrigerators) are subject to on-site technical inspection. If found dead-on-arrival (DOA), a 100% refund or instant replacement is initiated within 48 hours.

### 2.3 Fashion & Footwear
- **Eligibility:** 14-day hassle-free return or exchange for sizing discrepancies and aesthetic dissatisfaction. Items must be unworn with tags attached.

### 2.4 Beauty, Groceries & Personal Care
- **Non-Returnable:** Due to hygiene and health regulations, opened cosmetics, skincare serums, and grocery items cannot be returned unless delivered in damaged condition or past expiration date.

## 3. Refund Processing Time & Payment Channels
- **UPI & Instant Wallets:** 24 to 48 hours post return approval.
- **Credit / Debit Cards:** 5 to 7 business days depending on the issuing bank.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Refund transferred directly to verified bank account via NEFT/IMPS within 3 business days.

## 4. Refund Financial Impact & Loss Governance
Any refund resulting from confirmed merchant defect or logistics mishandling will have shipping fees and reverse logistics absorbed by DataMind corporate accounts. For customer-initiated returns outside defect claims, courier handling charges of ₹150 are deducted from the net refund balance.
