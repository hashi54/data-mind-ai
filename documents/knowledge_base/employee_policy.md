# Corporate Employee & Workplace Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-2024-V2  
**Department:** Human Resources & Legal  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  

---

## 1. Remote Work & Home Office Setup
Full-time employees are eligible for a one-time home office reimbursement of up to ₹25,000 for ergonomic chairs, secondary monitors, and desk accessories. Monthly broadband stipend of ₹1,500 is credited automatically with payroll.

## 2. Travel & Expense Reimbursement Guidelines
- **Domestic Travel:** Economy flight booking permitted for distances exceeding 500 km. Hotel allowance up to ₹4,500/night in Tier-1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru) and ₹3,000/night in Tier-2 cities.
- **Per Diem:** Daily food allowance of ₹1,200 per day during active business travel.
- **Submission Window:** Expense receipts must be filed within 30 days of trip completion.

## 3. Annual Leave & Wellness Policy
- All regular staff receive 24 paid annual leaves, 12 casual leaves, and 10 national/state public holidays. Up to 15 unused annual leaves may be encashed at the end of the fiscal year.
- Comprehensive health insurance cover of ₹7,50,000 provided for employee, spouse, and up to two dependent children.
