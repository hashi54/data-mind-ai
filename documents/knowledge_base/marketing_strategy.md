# Marketing Strategy & Growth Framework

**Document ID:** MKT-STR-2024-V2  
**Department:** Marketing & Growth  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024  

---

## 1. Channel Allocation & Blended CAC Targets
- **Google Search & Shopping Ads:** 40% of quarterly paid budget. Target Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): ₹420.
- **Meta (Instagram & Facebook):** 35% of budget. Target CAC: ₹380. High emphasis on lifestyle visual campaigns during festive months.
- **Email & Retention Automation:** 10% of budget. Dedicated re-engagement flows for inactive customer cohorts.
- **Influencer & Creator Partnerships:** 15% of budget with focus on Tech Reviewers and Fitness ambassadors.

## 2. Festive Campaign Planning & Seasonality
Major promotional spikes occur in Q3 and Q4:
- **May-June:** Summer Appliance Fest.
- **September:** Onam Kerala Festival promotion.
- **October-November:** Diwali Mega Savings Carnival.
- **December-January:** Year-End Clearance Sale.
