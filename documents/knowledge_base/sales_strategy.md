# Regional Sales Strategy & Discount Matrix

**Document ID:** STR-SLS-2024-V2  
**Department:** Sales & Business Development  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2024  

---

## 1. Regional Sales Objectives
- **South Region (Kerala, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu):** Target ₹45M annual gross revenue with focus on Smart Electronics and Ergonomic Living categories. Kerala and Maharashtra represent primary high-margin enterprise clusters.
- **West Region (Maharashtra, Gujarat):** Drive 25% YoY volume growth across Computing and Corporate Workstations.
- **North & East Regions:** Expand retail distribution and B2B educational institutional accounts.

## 2. Discount Approval Thresholds
- **Sales Executive:** Up to 5% discretionary discount on orders over ₹10,000.
- **Regional Sales Manager:** Up to 15% discount on commercial orders exceeding ₹50,000.
- **VP of Sales:** Discretionary approvals above 15% for enterprise procurement agreements exceeding ₹5,00,000.

## 3. Commission Structure
Sales representatives receive a 3.5% baseline commission on net settled revenue for transactions maintaining a minimum 22% gross profit margin.
